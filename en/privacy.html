Privacy Policy

For iOS version 1.0 · Updated October 4, 2026

In short: Clickety Mart has no account or developer-operated server. Version 1.0 does not collect personal data, show advertising, use analytics or crash-reporting services, or track you across apps or websites.

1. Data handled by the developer

PlayCubes does not collect personal information from Clickety Mart. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or other identifier. It contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or cross-app tracking SDK.

2. On-device game data

The game stores progress, unlocked items, settings, and local purchase-entitlement state in its on-device app container so that the game can work. This information is not sent to a PlayCubes server. Removing the app may remove this local data. Apple device backup behavior is controlled by your device, Apple Account, and system settings.

3. In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple, not PlayCubes, handles your payment method, billing details, and Apple Account. The game receives StoreKit transaction and entitlement results to deliver or restore the item you purchased; it does not receive your full payment details.

4. Advertising and tracking

Version 1.0 displays no advertisements, has no rewarded-ad flow, does not use the Advertising Identifier, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Because PlayCubes does not receive or retain your game data on a developer-operated service, there is no developer account or server record to delete. You can remove local game data by using an in-game reset option when available or by deleting the app. Purchases remain associated with your Apple Account according to Apple’s App Store policies and can be restored when applicable.

6. Children’s privacy

Clickety Mart is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not collect personal data through version 1.0. A parent or guardian with a privacy question may contact us using the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, an account system, a developer-operated service, or another data practice, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before enabling that practice.

8. Contact
