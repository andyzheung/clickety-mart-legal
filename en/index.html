Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a non-consumable purchase?

Open the Shop or Settings screen and choose Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Restoring does not restore consumable coins that were already spent.

Why is a purchase still pending?

Apple processes all purchases. Wait for the App Store to complete the transaction, then reopen the Shop. If a purchase is cancelled or pending approval, the game will not grant its item until StoreKit confirms it.

How do I keep or reset my progress?

Progress and settings are stored on your device. Removing the app can remove this local data. To begin again, use the in-game reset option if available, or remove and reinstall the app after making sure you no longer need the local progress.

Why is an item not available?

Some cosmetics or upgrades require coins or a completed purchase. Check the Shop for the current requirement. The iOS 1.0 release has no advertisements or rewarded-ad option.
